Our platform makes high quality apps possiblewithout native development

Median’s powerful webview app development platform, native plugins, and solutions unlock your ability to deliver native apps without the traditional complexity of native code.

App Studio

Median’s App Studio forms the core of our webview app development platform. Whether you want to convert a website into an app, or build a full-feature native app powered by web content, you’ll find everything at your fingertips and will never need to leave your browser – except to test your app on a physical device.

Build and preview in seconds

Run your app instantly on browser-based simulators and easily install test builds on physical iOS and Android devices. Make changes to your app and immediately see them reflected across multiple simulator devices. No special tools or expertise required.

Collaborate and manage with ease

Invite team members to develop and test shared apps with access levels set based on role. Manage multiple apps and app versions with flexible functionality, such as a tool to copy specific configuration settings between different apps.

Test twice and ready, set, publish

Median makes it seamless to install and test your iOS and Android apps on physical devices. When you’re ready to publish to the Apple App Store and Google Play, we’ve got you covered., whether you’re publishing yourself, or leveraging our experienced team. We offer a 100% approval guarantee, too.

Full-feature apps

No basic wrapper apps here. Build a full-feature native app with Native Plugins. Incorporate native functionality like push notifications, haptic vibration, and Face ID/Biometric login – including SDK integrations for leading mobile vendors.

Powerful push notifications

Reach your users through the most direct and effective engagement channel available, with integrations available for most leading push providers. Don’t see your provider of choice listed? Get in touch - we’re always adding more.

Secure and convenient authentication

Enable biometric login with Face ID, Touch ID, and Android Biometrics to offer a frictionless login experience. Median also supports popular social login providers, including Google, Facebook, and Apple, as well as enterprise-grade authentication solutions like Auth0 and Microsoft Intune.

App analytics and optimization

Integrate native analytics from providers like Adjust, AppsFlyer, and Branch. Track and optimize critical metrics, from app installs through user engagement, to refine user acquisition strategies and maximize your app’s growth.

Median JavaScript Bridge

Don't let lack of iOS and Android development expertise hold you back. Easily integrate native features without writing native code using the Median JavaScript Bridge.

Enhanced native app functionality

Take advantage of native device capabilities and features offered by Median. Support content sharing from your app to other apps, control screen brightness, display PDF documents in an embedded viewer with support for printing, and much more.

Dynamic native navigation

Create an intuitive user experience by incorporating native navigation menus that adjust dynamically. Customize and adjust menus based on user states, such as displaying different options for logged-in versus logged-out users.

Using a JavaScript framework?

Experience a familiar, productive development environment and confidently build high-performing native apps leveraging your existing expertise. Our NPM package is designed for advanced web developers using frameworks like React and Vue.js.
